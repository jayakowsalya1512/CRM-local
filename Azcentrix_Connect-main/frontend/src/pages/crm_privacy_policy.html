Privacy Policy

Azcentrix Connect / DigitCRM SaaS

Effective date: June 18, 2026

Ascent e Digit Solutions Private Limited ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Azcentrix Connect and DigitCRM SaaS service (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when authorized users access the Service through the web or mobile app.

The Service is a business CRM used by organizations. In many cases, your employer or the organization that provided your account controls the data entered into the Service. We process that data on their behalf and under their instructions, subject to our agreements and applicable law.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app advertising.

1. Information We Collect

Account and profile data

  • Name, username, email address, phone number, employee code, role, department, designation, branch, region, state, group, and reporting manager details.
  • Profile images or avatar URLs if your organization enables them.
  • Credentials and security data such as password hashes, login challenge tokens, trusted device tokens, PIN-related credentials, and password reset records.

Business and CRM data

  • Customer, lead, opportunity, tender, quotation, work order, approval, competitor, OEM, and report data entered by authorized users.
  • Business contact details such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, notes, payment terms, shipping details, and related documents.
  • Travel, attendance, expense, and approval workflow data where those modules are enabled for your tenant.

Device, session, and usage data

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, device label, user agent, login time, logout time, session identifiers, and activity logs.
  • Security events such as failed login attempts, captcha usage, account lockouts, device trust events, and audit trail records.
  • Error logs and diagnostic information needed to keep the Service reliable and secure.

Location data

  • If your organization enables the mobile location feature, we may collect latitude and longitude that your device submits while you are signed in.
  • You can usually control location access through your device settings, subject to your organization's policies.

Files and attachments

  • Documents, PDFs, spreadsheets, images, receipts, and other files you upload for CRM workflows, RFP analysis, travel claims, tenders, or approvals.
  • Metadata associated with those files, such as file name, size, type, upload time, and uploader.

Push notifications and email

  • Push notification tokens and device identifiers if you enable notifications.
  • Email addresses and message content needed to send account alerts, reset links, approvals, reminders, or other service messages.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • To authenticate users, manage sessions, and protect accounts from unauthorized access.
  • To store and process CRM records, approvals, documents, reports, and related business workflows.
  • To send service notifications, security alerts, password reset messages, and push notifications when enabled.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and technical issues.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve product performance and reliability.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

3. How We Share Information

  • Your organization and its administrators: Data entered into the Service is visible to authorized users within the tenant or account that provides your access.
  • Service providers: We may share data with hosting, database, storage, logging, email, and push-notification providers that help us run the Service.
  • Automated document processing: Some uploaded documents may be processed by internal automated tools to extract fields or support CRM workflows.
  • Legal and compliance: We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users, or systems.
  • Business transfers: If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • We store tenant data in tenant-specific databases and file storage locations.
  • We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as access controls, password hashing, session management, and encrypted transport where supported.
  • No system is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored through the Service.
  • We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or support your organization’s retention needs.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

  • The web app may use cookies, browser local storage, or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and improve your experience.
  • The mobile app may store similar session or preference information on your device as needed to keep the Service working.

6. Your Choices

  • You may be able to update or correct your profile information through the app or through your organization’s administrator.
  • You can revoke notification or location permissions through your device settings, if those features are available on your device.
  • You can sign out of the Service to end the current session, and you can clear browser storage if you are using the web app.
  • If you are an end user, some requests to access, correct, or delete data may need to go through your organization because it controls the tenant account.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for business users and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Third-Party Services

  • We may use Firebase Cloud Messaging or similar services to deliver push notifications.
  • Your organization may configure SMTP or email services to send notices, password resets, and approvals.
  • Depending on configuration, the Service may use internal analytics, logging, or monitoring tools to keep the platform reliable and secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above and post the revised version on this page.
